# Badge of Millhaven

## Premise

You are **Officer Dana Reyes**, first week on the **Millhaven** police force.
A delivery driver's story doesn't fit the pharmacy break-in. Follow the evidence
to a warehouse on Pier Road, make the arrest and bring back a case that sticks.

## Tone

A small-town police procedural with humor from paperwork and a sergeant who
has seen everything twice. Listening and connecting evidence win the day. PG-13.

## Art direction

Red brick, blue-and-white patrol cars and greenish office lights. Quiet streets,
readable shopfronts, an occasional blink from the radio.

## Protagonist

Dana wears a navy uniform, cap and duty belt. Alert, composed and recognisable
from every direction. Travel between locations is simple.

## Starting room

**The precinct front desk.** Dana has a badge and radio. Sergeant Prakash
has the assignment; the board keeps its details handy. Start with a conversation
and a useful lead.

## Lexicon

- Verbs: look, talk, read, take/get, show, check, radio/call, photograph
- Nouns: badge, driver, pharmacy, bottle, ledger, warehouse, suspect, report

## Point table (max 215)

Build a fair investigation around these milestones. Invent the evidence
and connections; award each achievement once.

| Points | Milestone                                                 |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 10     | Receive the assignment.                                   |
| 25     | Check the delivery driver's story and find a discrepancy. |
| 30     | Document the pharmacy break-in and collect evidence.      |
| 40     | Connect the records and witness account to the warehouse. |
| 60     | Call backup and make an arrest supported by the evidence. |
| 50     | File the complete case and finish Dana's first shift.     |

## Beats

1. A small district: precinct, records office, street, pharmacy and warehouse.
   New evidence gives Dana reasons to revisit familiar people.
2. Build toward the warehouse confrontation, then bring the story back to
   Prakash's desk. Let Dana ask for reminders and correct incomplete paperwork.

## NPC seeds

- **Sergeant Prakash** — has a tray for forms completed in the wrong tray
- **Officer Bud Bukowski** — partner, talks too much, brave when it counts
- **The pharmacist** — frightened of the delivery company
- **Dispatch** — patient, dry, happy to repeat a detail

## Deaths

- Bud warns Dana before entering the warehouse alone. Ignoring him can end the case.
- Missing evidence is something to go back for; paperwork can be corrected.
