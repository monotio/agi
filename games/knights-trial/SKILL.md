# Knight's Trial

## Premise

You are **Wenna**, third squire to King Brannoc of **Thornwall**, a kingdom
so faded the mapmakers have stopped drawing it. Recover the **Three Bright
Things** — an underwater Bell, an undying Ember and a wandering Map — and
bring them home before the kingdom's last candle burns out.

## Tone

A kind storybook adventure with dry wit. Curiosity gets Wenna further than a
sword. The candles give the story urgency, but leave the player time to explore. PG.

## Art direction

Green meadows, blue water, a gray castle and smoke from a crooked chimney.
Clear paths, quiet countryside, something worth looking at in every room.

## Protagonist

Wenna has short brown hair, a rust-red tunic and sleeves slightly too big for
her. A recognisable face and a steady, unhurried walk.

## Starting room

**The castle gate.** A royal notice, bread for the journey and a lit lantern.
The hall lies across the drawbridge; a meadow leads into the countryside.
An alligator watches from the moat. Give Wenna an easy first discovery.

## Lexicon

- Verbs: look, take/get, give, talk, read, use, light
- Nouns: notice, bread, lantern, heron, bell, ember, map, king

## Point table (max 230)

A loose route through the story. Invent the puzzles and clues connecting
these milestones. Award each once.

| Points | Milestone                                                      |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 10     | Accept the king's errand.                                      |
| 20     | Help the heron and earn passage along the river.               |
| 40     | Recover the Bell from the gorge.                               |
| 40     | Persuade the salamander to part with its Ember.                |
| 40     | Solve the old woman's riddle and receive the Map.              |
| 60     | Bring all three treasures home and restore Thornwall.          |
| 20     | Optional: relight the three roadside candles before finishing. |

## Beats

1. A small countryside: castle, meadow, river gorge, stove hut and an old oak
   beside a talking well. Let Wenna follow the three errands in any order.
2. Kindness opens paths. Clues connect the people and places; each treasure
   brings a little light back. Returning all three completes the story.

## NPC seeds

- **King Brannoc** — tired, funny, determined to leave something worth inheriting
- **The old woman** — knits with map-thread
- **The salamander** — vain, easily impressed by polished brass
- **The heron** — would like to discuss the bread first

## Deaths

- Deliberately swimming with the alligator is unwise. Warn Wenna first.
- Wrong answers earn hints. Treasures survive experiments and the journey home.
