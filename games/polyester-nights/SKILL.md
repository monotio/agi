# Polyester Nights

## Premise

You are **Dale Dorsey**: 44, divorced twice, salesman of the month (March
1987), wearing his one good white suit. The **Golden Coyote** lounge in
**Neon Gulch** is raffling two travel tickets tonight. Dale has $40 and
would quite like to be somewhere else. Win them and choose some company,
or a longer trip alone.

## Tone

Affectionate late-night comedy. Dale tries too hard, means well and finds
better company when he starts listening. Warm, mildly embarrassing, PG-13.

## Art direction

Deep blue streets, magenta neon and warm amber interiors. Clear pavements
beneath the signs; an occasional flicker, a little reflected light.

## Protagonist

White suit, open collar, gold-ish chain and a hopeful comb-over. Dale walks
slightly stiffly. A borrowed blue tracksuit later changes his clothes, not his face.

## Starting room

**Outside the Golden Coyote.** Tiny, the bouncer, is wrestling with a crooked
admission sign. Helping him should get Dale inside. A diner and taxi rank
promise a night that can go several ways.

## Lexicon

- Verbs: look, talk, read, help, take/get, give, show, play, dance
- Nouns: sign, tiny, velvet, microphone, pool, taxi, tourists, suit, ticket

## Point table (max 200)

One possible evening. Invent the small mishaps and favors that connect
these milestones. Each award is earned once.

| Points | Milestone                                                       |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 10     | Help Tiny and get into the lounge.                              |
| 20     | Help Velvet get her performance going.                          |
| 20     | Win a fair pool challenge and earn a raffle entry.              |
| 20     | Get the stranded tourists on their way.                         |
| 30     | Survive a laundry mishap and recover Dale's good suit.          |
| 40     | Complete the evening's errands and win the travel tickets.      |
| 40     | Choose company or a solo trip, bringing the evening to a close. |
| 20     | Optional: brave the dance floor while Velvet sings.             |

## Beats

1. A few lively places: lounge, pool room, diner, taxi rank and laundry.
   Favors bring people together and give Dale reasons to return.
2. Make the pool challenge learnable and the errands affordable. Hold the
   draw until the player is ready; leave time to explore and talk.
3. Velvet has her own evening and a say in the invitation. Travelling alone
   should feel like a good ending too.

## NPC seeds

- **Tiny** — an enormous bouncer who would rather somebody fixed the sign
- **Velvet** — singer, tired microphone, good sense of humor
- **The tourists** — stranded but trying to enjoy themselves
- **The laundry attendant** — lends Dale a tracksuit without commenting on it

## Deaths

- Mostly small embarrassments with another chance to try.
- The player chooses when to end the evening.
