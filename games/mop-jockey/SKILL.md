# Mop Jockey

## Premise

You are **Pip Scrubb**, sanitation technician third class aboard **Gleam-9**.
The **Vlorg Conglomerate** has invaded, abducted the command staff and seized
everything except the custodial deck. Retake the station with a mop, a bucket
and a better understanding of the plumbing.

## Tone

Blue-collar space comedy. The invaders have excellent uniforms and have not
found the light switches. Useful work solves increasingly important problems. PG.

## Art direction

White bulkheads, teal trim, hazard-yellow machinery. Broad corridors and
obvious spills. The cleaning drone bobs gently; the guards mostly look bored.

## Protagonist

Pip wears a gray jumpsuit and orange sash, with untidy hair and a broad
mustache. A comfortable walk; the mop comes out when there's work to do.

## Starting room

**Custodial Deck C.** A mop, bucket and wet-floor sign. The lift and intercom
are dead; a floor buffer occupies the socket marked COMMUNICATIONS. Give Pip
a small maintenance problem before revealing the invasion upstairs.

## Lexicon

- Verbs: look, take/get, unplug, talk, read, mop/clean, use, open
- Nouns: mop, bucket, sign, buffer, intercom, guards, order, duct, ship

## Point table (max 250)

Turn ordinary custodial jobs into the steps of a rescue. Invent the puzzles
between these milestones; award each once.

| Points | Milestone                                                        |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 10     | Collect the cleaning kit.                                        |
| 20     | Restore communications and hear what's happened.                 |
| 30     | Outsmart the cargo guards with custodial equipment.              |
| 30     | Obtain a work order with surprisingly broad authority.           |
| 40     | Reach the brig through a maintenance route and rescue the staff. |
| 40     | Repair the docking machinery and send the invaders' ship away.   |
| 60     | Return control to the staff and give the final all-clear.        |
| 20     | Optional: finish the cleaning before claiming victory.           |

## Beats

1. Keep the station small: custodial deck, cargo, engineering, office, brig
   and command. Useful shortcuts make Pip's local knowledge matter.
2. Each repair opens another possibility. The rescued engineer helps with
   the final job; Pip gets the credit when the station is safe.

## NPC seeds

- **M.O.P.** — station computer; literal, bored, mildly pro-Pip
- **Sub-Director Vlemm** — invader, middle manager, allergic to soap
- **Chief Engineer Okafor** — brig prisoner, furious, useful
- **Biscuit** — a defective but loyal cleaning drone

## Deaths

- An airlock warning deserves attention. Forcing it earns an unplanned departure.
- Getting caught means a trip back to the closet, tools intact. Try again.
