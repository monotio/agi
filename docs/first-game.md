# Build your first game

Open [AGI IS HERE](https://agi.monotio.com/) and choose **Make a new game**.
Enter a name, select **Starter**, then choose **Start building**. Your hero starts
in the Meadow, with the game running beside the parts list.

1. Open **Meadow → PICTURE** in the parts list. Select the first item (the sun),
   give the canvas keyboard focus, and press the down arrow to move it.
   **Draft saved** confirms browser storage; the game keeps its last update. Click the top-bar room action:
   it shows a filled update icon, with the tooltip **Update and restart Meadow**. The game now shows your edit. **Undo** reverses it; update again to
   apply the reversal.
2. Open **Meadow → LOGIC**. Find the reply beginning “You stand in a sunny
   clearing” and replace that message with “Welcome to my first game.” Click
   **Update and restart Meadow**, then focus the game and type `look`. Your new
   reply appears. Press Enter to dismiss it. A source error appears in
   **Problems** and leaves the last working build running.
3. Open the hero under **VIEWS**. Use **Pencil** to change a pixel in a cel,
   then **Update and restart Meadow**. Move the hero in the game to see its
   animation. **Focus** expands the editor; toggle it again or press Escape
   twice to return to the workspace.
4. Open **WORDS** and enter `look at the tree` in the sentence tester to see
   its meanings. Return to Meadow's LOGIC and choose **Answer a sentence** in
   the room actions. Enter `hello` and a reply, then choose **Add**. Update,
   focus the game and type `hello` to see the reply.
5. Click **Saved** to open **History**. Enter a **Version name** and choose
   **Name this version** to name a checkpoint. Naming waits for the visible
   edits to save, including source with errors. Undo and Redo work across editors.
6. Switch to **Play** for the game at full size. Open **Settings → This game →
   Download…**. Choose **Project file** for a private backup with edits and
   available chats, images and progress, or **Playable game** for AGI resources
   to share. Either ZIP opens again with **Add game**.

Continue with the [Create workspace guide](create.md) or the
[LOGIC language reference](logic-language.md). **Agent** can help when you connect
a provider; **Review** lets you inspect and approve its proposed changes.
