Bundled UI fonts
================

The browser UI includes Geist and Geist Mono 1.7.2, Copyright 2024 The Geist
Project Authors (https://github.com/vercel/geist-font), under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. These fonts and their derivatives are not covered by this project's
MIT license.

AGI Geist is a renamed Latin subset of Geist with static symbol outlines from
Inter 4.1, Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter),
and Noto Sans Math and Noto Sans Symbols 2, Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors
(https://github.com/notofonts/math and https://github.com/notofonts/symbols).
Those outlines are also licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Geist Mono is a Latin subset of the upstream variable font.

The complete upstream OFL notices ship in app/public/licenses/geist-OFL.txt,
inter-OFL.txt, noto-math-OFL.txt and noto-symbols-OFL.txt. Source identities and
subset generation are documented in CONTRIBUTING.md. The notice also ships as
app/public/fonts/NOTICE.txt. The fonts apply to browser UI and code editors;
the interpreter's original project 8x8 game font remains separate.
